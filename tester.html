How to Throw (Recommended: Arc Snap)

  1. Grip (Grab): Touch your thumb tip to your index fingertip (or thumb + index + middle like holding a dart) in front of the camera so the green line lights up.
  2. Wind up (Cock): Keep pinching and pull your hand back/down slightly toward your chest or raise it to eye level with your wrist cocked back.
  3. Snap (Flick): Swing smoothly from your elbow and snap your forearm/wrist up and slightly forward toward the phone in a soft 45° arc.
  4. Release: Open your fingers while your hand is still snapping upward/forward — not after your hand stops at the top.

Why this matches real beer pong: Real players throw a soft arc rather than a flat fastball so the ball drops steeply into the cup opening instead of bouncing off the rim. The elbow stays steady as the pivot while the forearm and wrist create a repeatable lob.
